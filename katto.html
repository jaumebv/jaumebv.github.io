Overview

Katto: Rising Tides is a stylised 3D platformer developed in Unreal Engine 5 by Arcadia Games. Built around fluid movement, expressive combat and a cohesive Mediterranean-inspired aesthetic, the project took us from early prototypes to a public Steam launch in seven months.

My focus was squarely on runtime infrastructure and gameplay feel: I designed the custom level-streaming system that kept memory and frame times predictable across zones, and engineered the AI and combat behaviour of the game’s two primary enemies.

Engineering breakdown

Systems I owned.

STREAMING

Custom Level-Streaming System

Designed and implemented a runtime level-streaming layer tailored to our designers’ workflow, letting areas load and unload seamlessly as the player traverses the world.

  • Predictable runtime budgets: Keeps only active and adjacent zones resident in memory, cutting draw-call and actor overhead during exploration.
  • Designer-friendly iteration: Exposed clean volume triggers and sublevel boundaries so designers could rearrange encounters without breaking streaming state.
UE5 C++Runtime streamingMemory budget
COMBAT AI

Enemy Logic & Combat Behaviour

Programmed the AI state logic and combat loops for the two main enemy archetypes, integrating animation montages and telegraph windows directly with the art team.

  • Readable telegraphs: Synchronised wind-up frames, hitboxes and hit-reactions so attacks feel fair and responsive under platforming movement.
  • Tight state transitions: Smooth blending across idle, patrol, aggro, attack and recovery states without animation locks.

Cross-Department Collaboration

Worked daily alongside 7 programmers, 2 designers and 7 artists (plus 5 musicians), bridging engineering constraints with visual and gameplay polish up to release.
