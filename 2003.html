Overview

2003 is a minimalist tile-based puzzle game built in Unity and prepared for App Store distribution on iPhone via Xcode. Created alongside Juan Avilés and Nacho Avilés, the project treats UI itself as the core gameplay medium.

Every menu screen, tile slide and state change was tuned for rhythm and clarity—turning a compact puzzle ruleset into a cohesive, tactile mobile experience.
